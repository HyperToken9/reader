# Glossary: the read-aloud extension

Words for what the reader sees and does. Settled while charting the map, 2026-11-10.

**Main content**
The part of a web page that is the thing being read: the article, the chapter, the documentation page. Navigation, ads, sidebars, cookie banners and comments are not main content.

**Sentence spotlight**
The mark on the sentence being spoken, with the rest of the main content dimmed around it. It sits on the page's own text, where the text already is.
_Avoid:_ "highlight" on its own, which is ambiguous with highlights the reader or the site make.

**Word cursor**
The mark on the word being spoken, inside the sentence spotlight.

**Two layers**
The sentence spotlight and the word cursor together: what the reader sees while listening.

**Read from here**
Start reading at the sentence the reader chose, and carry on through the main content to its end.

**Read this**
Read only the text the reader selected, then stop.

**Reading pill**
The small control that floats over the page only while something is being read: play and pause, a sentence back or forward, speed, and close.

**Follow the voice**
The page scrolls by itself to keep the sentence spotlight in view.
